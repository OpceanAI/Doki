## 1. About Whaley

Whaley is OpceanAI's official mascot — an axolotl character representing the Doki project ecosystem. Despite being an axolotl, Whaley identifies as a whale out of profound respect for container technology, serving as the official guardian of the Doki runtime environment.

Whaley embodies curiosity, resilience, and the collaborative spirit of open-source development.

## 4. Usage in Open-Source Projects

You are encouraged to take **artistic inspiration** from Whaley to create your own original mascot for your fork, derivative project, or independent open-source initiative.

This means you may:

- study Whaley's visual design, character traits, personality, and artistic direction;
- create new mascots influenced by Whaley's aesthetic, style, or thematic elements;
- develop your own unique character that captures the spirit of Whaley without being a direct reproduction.

**Your mascot should be:**

- substantially your own original artistic work;
- noticeably distinct from Whaley in visual presentation, not a direct copy or minimal modification;
- accompanied by a note (if desired) acknowledging Whaley as inspiration.

This approach encourages diversity, originality, and creative expression in the open-source ecosystem while maintaining a visual and cultural connection to the Doki project family.

**Example of encouraged use:**

> "Our fork features [your custom mascot name], an original character inspired by the creative direction of OpceanAI's Whaley mascot."

**What we ask you not to do:**

> "Our fork uses Whaley, the mascot from Doki by OpceanAI." (in a closed-source project without attribution)

> "Our fork's mascot is Whaley." (when you've simply copied the artwork without modification)

Instead, create your own character. We believe your fork deserves its own identity.
